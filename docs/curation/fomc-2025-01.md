## How these candidates were chosen (same mechanical rule for every meeting)

Selection does not look at how the meeting went. It takes, for this `as_of`:

1. the policy statement of the previous scheduled FOMC meeting (December 17-18, 2024);
2. the minutes of that meeting (released 2025-01-08);
3. the BLS CPI news release that was current at `as_of` (read from the latest Wayback capture of
   `bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm` taken before `as_of`);
4. the BLS Employment Situation news release current at `as_of` (same method, `empsit.nr0.htm`);
5. up to 4 Board of Governors speeches or testimonies from the Board's own feeds
   (`federalreserve.gov/json/ne-speeches.json`, `ne-testimony.json`), dated after the previous statement and
   before `as_of`, whose body text mentions "monetary policy", "policy rate" or "federal funds rate" at least
   5 times in total, taking the most recent qualifying item per speaker, newest first. One item per speaker
   spreads the set across different policymakers without the curator choosing by viewpoint.

Excerpt rule (no curator choice of "telling" sentences): statement = first two sentences after the
"For release at" line; minutes = the sentence that contains "outlook for monetary policy" plus the next one;
BLS = the release title line plus the first summary paragraph; speech/testimony = the first sentence that
mentions one of the three policy terms (skipping disclaimer sentences). Long excerpts are clipped at about
75 words and marked `[...]`. A speech excerpt can start with the section heading printed
above the sentence on the page (for example "Monetary Policy ..."), because the page puts both in one block. Every excerpt was read from the Wayback capture listed, which was taken before
`as_of`; Federal Reserve excerpts were also compared with the live page fetched 2026-11-13.

**Not covered by the rule (gaps the finance owner may want to fill by hand, with the same checks):** Reserve
Bank presidents' speeches (published on 12 separate sites), the previous meeting's press-conference transcript
and Summary of Economic Projections, PCE inflation (BEA), and news previews. News previews were not drafted:
the main wires are paywalled or block automated reads, and choosing among them is where hindsight bias would
enter most easily.
